Our Story

Every piece is handcrafted by hand

Artisanal, handcrafted goods that blend traditional craftsmanship with a modern aesthetic — bringing beauty, warmth, and story into your home.

Slow stitches, one piece at a time

Every rug, tapestry and napkin that leaves Mulberry Tales starts the same way — a hoop, a needle, and hours of hand stitching. Nothing is mass-produced; each motif, from a hand-painted parrot to a fully embroidered dodo, is worked by hand from start to finish.

"Every piece can be made to order — custom sizes, colours, and motifs, built around your space."
